Jorden Lacy Jackson Quietly Releases a Music Trilogy

Los Angeles-based writer, mystic, and technologist releases debut acapella songs with no announcement, audience, explanation, or apology

LOS ANGELES - June 15, 2026 --- Jorden Lacy Jackson released three raw acapella songs into the world. Just her voice with a cathedral echo and a title that arrives like a confession: i love you... (i need to scream)

The trilogy, i love you v1, i love you v2, and i love you... (i need to scream), is available now on all major streaming platforms.

About The Music

The trilogy moves from vulnerability to revelation. i love you v1 arrives raw and intimate with her voice barely above a whisper. i love you v2 deepens the emotional register. Sensual and ethereal, the kind of song that finds you in a quiet room and doesn't let go.

By the time i love you... (i need to scream) arrives, the music has evolved into something deeper and together the three songs tell one cohesive story that's still evolving after the music stops. It's the kind of music that finds emotions words can't reach and speaks them for you.

"I know these songs are not perfect," says Jackson. "They're not meant to be. I just wanted them to be true. I made them and published them in 48 hours without overthinking. I had to start somewhere. And after years of feeling blocked creatively, it felt really good to sing again."

Where To Listen

i love you... (i need to scream) and the full trilogy are available now on Spotify, Apple Music, and all major streaming platforms.

About Jorden Lacy Jackson

Jorden Lacy Jackson is a writer, mystic, and technologist based in Los Angeles. She has spent over a decade working at the intersection of storytelling, creativity, and technology. Her forthcoming body of work will be released later this year.
