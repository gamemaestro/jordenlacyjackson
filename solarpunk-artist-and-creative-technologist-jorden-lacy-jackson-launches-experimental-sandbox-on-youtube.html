Solarpunk Artist and Creative Technologist Jorden Lacy Jackson Launches Experimental Sandbox on YouTube

Jorden Lacy Jackson brings a decade of tech, storytelling, and mysticism to YouTube as she documents her messy journey of self-mastery and building towards a solarpunk future in real time

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

August 20, 2026

LOS ANGELES, CA — Jorden Lacy Jackson, solarpunk artist, creative technologist, and metaphysical researcher, announces the launch of her personal YouTube channel where viewers get a behind-the-scenes look at what it actually takes to build a creative and spiritual practice from the inside out.

The channel is described by Jackson as her sandbox: an unfiltered record of the divergent, messy, nonlinear process of discovery, shipping, launching, and mastering in real time. Early episodes explore themes including faith, miracles and synchronicity, solarpunk dreaming, nonlinear workflows, and the practical realities of building authentically, alone.

Jackson brings over a decade of experience at the intersection of storytelling and technology, including a roles at The Walt Disney Company and Chipotle Mexican Grill. Her current work operates where ancient metaphysics meets modern technology, touching publishing, production, web development, solarpunk advocacy, and metaphysical practice.

"This channel is my personal sandbox," says Jackson. "The raw, unfiltered documentation of what it looks like to individuate and build something real from the inside out."

The personal channel serves as a companion to her growing institutional work. Old Ink Records, Jackson's solarpunk research and media institution, is forthcoming and will publish original histories, mysteries, and philosophical investigations through its YouTube channel and podcast network.

Her personal channel is live now at youtube.com/@jordenlacyjackson.

About Jorden Lacy Jackson

Jorden Lacy Jackson is a solarpunk artist, creative technologist, and metaphysical researcher based in Los Angeles. Her work spans publishing, media production, web development, and advocacy at the intersection of ancient wisdom and emerging technology.
