About

Jorden Lacy Jackson

Solarpunk artist, creative technologist, worldbuilder and writer. Los Angeles.

Jorden Lacy Jackson is a solarpunk artist, creative technologist, worldbuilder and writer based in Los Angeles. She spent over a decade at the intersection of storytelling and technology inside some of the world's most recognizable brands, including The Walt Disney Company and Chipotle Mexican Grill.

Her work treats solarpunk as more than an aesthetic. It is a lifestyle, a mindset and a practice of inner work: the belief that we are living between worlds, and that it's up to us to build the bridge between the old one and the new one.

That work runs in two directions. Solarpunk Sandbox, her personal YouTube channel, looks forward with dreams, experiments and blueprints for a solarpunk future. The Solarpunk Scriptorium at Old Ink Records, the research and record keeping institution she founded in 2026, looks back through Histories, Mysteries and the Comedies of our own lives.

She also writes and makes music. Her a cappella trilogy, i love you... (i need to scream), was released in June 2026.
