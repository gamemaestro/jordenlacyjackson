Old Ink Records Research and Record Keeping Institution Launches in Los Angeles on August 26, 2026

A publishing and research hub dedicated to the histories, mysteries, and potential futures of humanity

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

August 26, 2026

LOS ANGELES, CA — Old Ink Records officially launches today, August 26, 2026.

From the first female scribes of ancient Mesopotamia to the nuns who preserved civilization in medieval scriptoria, the keepers of records are revered for doing some of humanity's most important work. Old Ink Records exists to honor that lineage and continue it through original research, published histories, metaphysical inquiry, and tools for anyone curious about their own histories, mysteries, and records.

The institution publishes histories, mysteries, and investigations exploring the esoteric and philosophical threads connecting ancient wisdom to modern life; and potential futures, exploring what humanity's records reveal about where we are going.

The institution is founded by Jorden Lacy Jackson, writer, artist, creative technologist, and metaphysical researcher based in Los Angeles.

About Old Ink Records

Old Ink Records is a research and publishing institution exploring the histories, mysteries, and potential futures of humanity through the lens of sacred record keeping. oldinkrecords.com
