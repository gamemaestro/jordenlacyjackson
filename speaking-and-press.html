Talks

They Called It Soulless

Every new art technology has been called soulless, from recorded music in 1906 to AI today. What that pattern tells artists about the tools they fear, and how to build with new technology without losing the soul of the work.

Legacy Work Starts Young

Record keeping and legacy work aren't only for the end of life. Why people in transition, starting a new chapter or finding their purpose, should start keeping their own record now, and how to begin.

Short bio

Jorden Lacy Jackson is a solarpunk artist, creative technologist, worldbuilder and writer based in Los Angeles. After more than a decade in storytelling and technology at brands including The Walt Disney Company and Chipotle Mexican Grill, she created Solarpunk Sandbox and founded Old Ink Records, a solarpunk research and record keeping institution.
